# Project Prototype Plan – Student Support Appointments

## Selected User Journey

**Actor:** Student.

**Goal:** A student wants to find a suitable support service and request an appointment.

## Proposed Screens

1. **Browse Support Services** – View the available student support services.
2. **Service Details** – View information about the selected support service.
3. **Check Appointment Availability** – Explore available appointment times. Exact availability rules need confirmation.
4. **Request an Appointment** – Enter the information needed to request an appointment.
5. **Review Appointment Request** – Check the request details before submitting.
6. **Request Submitted** – Show that the request has been submitted. The confirmation process needs clarification.

## Open Questions

- How is appointment availability displayed?
- Can students choose a specific staff member?
- Can students cancel or change an appointment?
- Does submitting a request confirm the appointment or require staff approval?

## Purpose

The prototype will explore the student appointment-request journey, identify missing information and help review the usability of the proposed system.
